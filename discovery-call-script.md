# Discovery Call Script

## Opening
"Thanks for taking the call. I work with dental clinics that want more booked consultations without increasing front-desk workload. I noticed a common issue in many clinics: missed calls, slow responses, and inconsistent follow-up. We help fix that with AI-powered inquiry handling and booking automation."

## Pain check
"How are new patient leads being handled right now? Are missed calls or delayed replies a problem?"

## Proof
"The goal is simple: when a patient reaches out, they get a fast, professional response immediately. That often means more booked consultations and less lead leakage."

## Offer
"We can set up a system that captures the inquiry, qualifies the patient, and either books the appointment or hands it to your team. We can start with a quick setup and then optimize monthly based on performance."

## Close
"Would it be worth doing a 10-minute audit to see where your current intake process is leaking leads?"

## If they say yes
"Great. I’ll send over a short overview and we can schedule the audit."

## If they say no
"No problem. If your priorities change later, I’d still be glad to share a quick way to improve patient intake."
